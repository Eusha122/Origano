OREGANO LOGO KIT
================
Logo typeface: Kaushan Script (by Pablo Impallari, SIL Open Font License), converted to outlines.
You do not need the font installed: all files are vector shapes.

Formats
- SVG: vector, for web, apps and design tools (Figma, Illustrator).
- PDF: vector, for print shops.
- PNG: high resolution with transparent background (wordmarks 4000 px wide, marks 2048 px, icons 1024 px).

Folders
1-Wordmark-transparent     Main logo in plum, champagne, cream, white, black
2-Wordmark-on-background   Ready-made logo on brand backgrounds (social, banners)
3-O-mark-transparent       The O alone, for small spaces and patterns
4-App-icon                 App store icons (square + rounded) and circular profile pictures

Colours
Plum       #3D1A33   RGB 61, 26, 51
Champagne  #EBC9A0   RGB 235, 201, 160
Cream      #F8EDE6   RGB 248, 237, 230

Rules
- Plum logo on light backgrounds; champagne or cream on plum.
- Keep clear space of at least the height of the "O" around the logo.
- Minimum size: 80 px wide on screen, 25 mm in print.
- Do not stretch, recolour, outline, add shadows or retype the logo.
